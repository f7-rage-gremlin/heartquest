The Game

Not just a dating app with a game skin. A real RPG where dating is the endgame.

⚔️ Monster Hunting

Fight roaming monsters for XP, gold, and item drops. Every battle makes you stronger. No paying for power — only skill and time invested.

💰 Earn Items by Playing

Gold, items, and rare loot are earned through gameplay. The longer you've played, the more powerful your gear. Veterans get detection advantages.

🏹 Proximity Notifications

Get alerted when a match enters your zone. Set your range — from 100m to 5km. Upgrade your Antenna item to expand it.

🪞 Magic Mirror

See your nearby match's photo before you meet. An item earned through gameplay that removes the guesswork while keeping the adventure.

👥 Team Up with Rivals

Hostile to friendly in one tap. Team up with your rivals to fight tougher monsters together, then split the loot. Trust is earned.

🧥 Cloak of Deception

A rare item that lets you appear friendly while remaining hostile. Use it wisely — other players can craft detection items to see through the veil.

⚔️ Fight Your Rivals

Spot rivals within range based on your stats and items. Attack them for damage. Knock them out and you'll steal one of their items.

🔗 Mega Items

Combine two items to forge a Mega Item with greater power. Only veterans have the components to craft these.

🎯 Ice Breaker Challenges

Guess each other's answers, solve riddles together, complete fun tasks. Earn XP and unlock abilities for your duo.

💎 Legendary Items

Only obtainable when two players meet and combine their finds. The rarer the encounter, the more powerful the item.
